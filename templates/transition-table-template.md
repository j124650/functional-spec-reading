## Interpretation notes

- `EXPLICITLY FORBIDDEN` means the source prohibits the transition.
- `UNSPECIFIED` means the source does not define the behavior.
- `AMBIGUOUS` means the source supports more than one interpretation.
- `CANDIDATE` means a plausible transition has been inferred but needs confirmation.

Do not represent an unspecified transition as forbidden.
